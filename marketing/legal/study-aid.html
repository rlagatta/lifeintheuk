Study-aid notice

Life in the UK Atlas is an independent study aid for people preparing for the Life in the UK test (British citizenship or Indefinite Leave to Remain). It is not an official test, not a government service, and not affiliated with the UK Home Office, TSO, or HM Government.

Primary study grounding is Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents, 3rd edition. Practice questions are handbook-aligned study material, not live official exam questions.

Readiness scores, badges and pass/fail results inside the app are study indicators only. They do not guarantee a result on the official test. Always verify current requirements and book via GOV.UK.
